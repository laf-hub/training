Food Hygiene and Safety Course

This free course covers everything expected of a Level 2 Food Safety and Hygiene programme for catering and retail staff, structured in line with CPD good-practice principles (clear learning outcomes, defined study time, and an end assessment).

Important: this is not a regulated qualification issued by an Ofqual-recognised awarding body, and it has not (yet) been submitted to a formal CPD accreditation body. Treat it as solid internal training and a genuine self-assessed knowledge check, not as a substitute for an accredited Level 2 certificate. See "About accreditation" below for what that would involve.
How this works: work through the 10 modules below in order (each is a short slide deck, roughly 5–10 minutes (the exact estimate is shown on each module's opening slide)), then sit the final exam. You need 70% or higher to pass and receive a certificate.

About accreditation

This course is designed to CPD-aligned standards but has not (yet) been submitted to a formal CPD accreditation body. Formal accreditation (e.g. via the CPD Certification Service or CPD Standards Office) typically costs from around £300 and takes 6–10 weeks to process. That step can be taken once the content is finalised and reviewed.
