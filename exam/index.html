Course Exam

30 questions, drawn at random from a larger pool covering all 10 modules, with question and answer order shuffled for each attempt. You need 70% or higher to pass. There's no time limit, so take your time and go back to a module if you want to check something first.

This is a self-marked, unproctored exam with no identity verification. It's a genuine check of your own understanding, but it isn't equivalent to a supervised, invigilated assessment, and a passing certificate should be read with that in mind.
